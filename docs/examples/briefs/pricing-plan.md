Company: Larkspur Analytics, a B2B finance analytics SaaS (made-up example). 140 customers, mostly mid-size companies, annual contracts, average contract value 18,000 EUR, net revenue retention 104%.

Goal: grow revenue from existing customers in 2027 without raising list prices.

Decision to review: move all customers from per-seat pricing to usage-based pricing.

Plan:
1. New price metric: 0.90 EUR per report generated in Larkspur. No seat fees.
2. Announce the change by email on 1 December 2026. It applies to every contract that renews from 1 December 2026 on.
3. We modelled the change on our 20 largest customers: at their current report volume, revenue from them goes up 18%. We expect the same uplift across the base, so about +18% ARR in 2027.
4. No caps or committed-volume tiers: customers pay exactly for what they use, which is the fairest model.
5. Sales commission stays on the number of seats sold, as today, so the sales team needs no retraining.
6. Customer success will answer questions after the announcement.

Constraints: the finance buyers who sign our contracts approve annual budgets in October and November. Most contracts have a 60-day notice clause for price changes.
