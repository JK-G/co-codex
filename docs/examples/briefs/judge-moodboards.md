Decision to make: which of three visual directions should become the social media template of Larkspur Analytics, a made-up B2B data analytics software company.

Context:
- Audience: finance and operations managers at mid-size companies, mostly on LinkedIn.
- The template must let a marketing generalist (not a designer) publish 3 posts a week in a tool like Canva or Figma: stat posts, customer quotes, webinar announcements.
- No budget for regular photo shoots. Stock photography is allowed but disliked by the team.
- The brand must look credible to finance buyers and stand out in a crowded LinkedIn feed.

The three directions are moodboards in these image files. Open and look at each one before you judge:
1. ledger.png ("Ledger")
2. signal.png ("Signal")
3. field.png ("Field")

Judge them on: fit with the finance audience, distinctiveness in the feed, how easily a non-designer can keep producing posts, legibility and contrast of text on the post tiles (check the actual colour pairs you see), and risks. Rank all three, name the one you would pick, and say what must change in it before it is used. Put the ranking in the summary.
